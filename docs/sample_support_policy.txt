Support Policy and Escalation Notes

Support hours are Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM local time.
Urgent issues can be reported outside normal hours, but response time may be delayed.

Issue severity levels:
- Low: Minor inconvenience, workaround available
- Medium: Feature is broken for one user or one workflow
- High: Major workflow blocked for multiple users
- Critical: Service unavailable or data integrity at risk

Escalation process:
1. Confirm the issue and gather reproduction steps.
2. Check logs, recent deployments, and configuration changes.
3. Assign the ticket to the on-call engineer if severity is high or critical.
4. Update the user with a clear status and next check-in time.

Policy reminders:
- Never promise a fix before root cause is known.
- Always include the ticket number in follow-up messages.
- Preserve evidence such as screenshots, timestamps, and error messages.
